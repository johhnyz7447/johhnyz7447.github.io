Privacy policy

gmail-multi-mcp is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Gmail accounts.

  • It reads email and creates drafts only when the owner asks it to.
  • Access tokens stay on the owner's computer. Email content the owner asks about is processed by the owner's AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) to answer that request, and is not sold or shared with anyone else.
  • It does not send email; drafts are reviewed and sent by the owner in Gmail.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
